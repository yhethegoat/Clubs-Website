Environmental Club



Club Description


A student or community group dedicated to protecting nature, raising ecological awareness, and promoting sustainable daily habits



Purpose and Goals


  • Teach members and the public about conservation, preservation, and ecological restoration.
  • Lead practical local projects like campus clean-ups, tree planting, and recycling programs.
  • Develop leadership, teamwork, and social skills through group collaboration.


Activities and Teacher:


Members organize regular events to remove litter from school grounds, local parks, and nearby beaches. These events directly improve local habitats and prevent pollution from reaching ecosystems. Facilitated by Ms. Paredez




Requirements for becoming a member


  • Most maintain a 3.5 GPA
  • Bring own gloves
  • Can actively participate 2 times a week
