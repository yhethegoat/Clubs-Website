Rotary Club



Club Description


Our sports club provides a welcoming environment for athletes of all skill levels to train, compete, and improve their physical fitness. Through expert coaching and a vibrant community spirit, we help members achieve their personal health goals while building lasting friendships.



Purpose and Goals


  • Exercise Everyday for Students
  • Provide a fun way to get active
  • Promote heathy habits


Activities and Teacher:


A sports club offers structured athletic programs and regular training sessions to help members improve their physical fitness and skills. It also hosts competitive matches, social events, and recreational games to build a strong, active community. Facilitated by Mr. Riverol




Requirements for becoming a member


  • Most maintain a 3.5 GPA
  • Bring your water
  • Proper exercise attire
