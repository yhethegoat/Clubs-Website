Rotary Club



Club Description


The Robotics Club is a student group focused on learning about robotics, programming, engineering, and technology through hands-on projects and activities.



Purpose and Goals


  • Aims to improve students’ problem-solving, creativity, teamwork, and technical skills while encouraging an interest in STEM.
  • PRobotics Club is to provide students with opportunities to explore robotics and develop practical skills in a fun and collaborative environment.
  • Promoting understanding within technical robotic fields


Activities and Teacher:


Students build and program robots, work on team challenges, learn coding, and participate in robotics competitions and projects. Facilitated by: Mr. S. Pech




Requirements for becoming a member


  • Most maintain a 3.5 GPA
  • Loves Robotics
  • Can attend every meeting
